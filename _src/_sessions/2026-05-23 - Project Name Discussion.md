# Project Name Discussion

**Date:** 2026-05-23
**Context:** The working title "Family Archive" was identified as too narrow. The app's core functionality — browsing a raw directory of media and documents and adding database records about them — is not inherently family-specific. A better name should reflect what the app does, not what it currently holds.

---

## The Problem With "Family Archive"

"Family Archive" describes the *current dataset*, not the app. The application itself is a general-purpose local file cataloging and annotation tool. It could be used for any collection of files — a photography project, a business document store, a personal media library. The name should reflect that generality.

---

## Candidates Considered

| Name | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **Folio** | Clean, minimal. A folio is an organized collection. Implies curation without implying family or history. Works as both a noun and a product name. |
| **Codex** | More gravitas. Historically refers to an organized manuscript or collection. Fits an app that layers structured records on top of raw files. |
| **Annotate / Annotator** | Most literal — the core action of the app is annotating existing files with database records. Clear, purposeful, and domain-agnostic. |
| **Ledger** | Suggests systematic record-keeping. Works because the app doesn't reorganize files — it keeps a *record* alongside them. Slight archival tone without being family-specific. |
| **Chronicle** | Leans into the time-based nature of the content (dated filenames, historical media) without assuming family context. |
| **Curator** | Captures the user's role — curating a collection, not just browsing it. A curator organizes, annotates, and maintains. Fully domain-agnostic. |
| **Gloss** | A gloss is literally an annotation or explanatory note. Understated and precise. May be too obscure for general use. |
| **Notate** | Verb-as-name. Shorter than Annotator. Describes the act of adding notations to files in place. |

---

## Recommendation

**Folio** or **Curator** were identified as the strongest options:

- **Folio** reads as a polished product name, is short and memorable, and works for any kind of archive.
- **Curator** describes the user's role precisely and implies active engagement with the collection rather than passive browsing.

Neither name implies family history, and both would remain accurate if the app were used for any other type of media or document collection.

---

## No Decision Made

A final name was not chosen in this session. This document serves as a record of the options discussed. The working title remains "Family Archive" until a name is confirmed.
